  ReGLU and interactions
  - ReGLU never beats ReLU in either task; the corrected p-values are 0.37 and
    above.
  - Adding Teenhome helps both regression models by about +0.012, but only the
    uncorrected Wilcoxon test supports it.
  - The classification interaction gains are weak: +0.023 for LightGBM with
    pairs, Wilcoxon only.

  Likely reasons (not tested individually)
  - About 2,200 rows and 15–22 features is the regime where boosted trees
    usually win.
  - FT has about 18,600 parameters for about 1,800 training rows (fold-to-fold
    SD 0.04–0.06).
  - NAM is additive by design, so it cannot capture interactions unless pair
    subnets are added, and those didn't help.

  An unfairness in the comparison that favours the curriculum models: the
  library models get 30 Optuna trials. The FT and NAM use one fixed recipe, and
  only the epoch count is selected. That doesn't change the sign of the result,
  but it is the main thing option (b) would address.

  Caveats:
  - The best curriculum model is a V5 variant (LightGBM with extra features), so
    picking the best of several on the same folds slightly favours it. The
    plain-baseline rows above guard against that.
  - Identical-feature FT scores differ by about 0.01 F1 between V4_1 and V5_1,
    probably from the dropout random stream, so treat gaps of about 0.01 as
    noise.
