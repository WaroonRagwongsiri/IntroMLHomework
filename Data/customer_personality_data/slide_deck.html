05 Classification

Relevance Score — How It's Computed

Two methods, chosen by feature type, both scored against Response (0/1).

Point-biserial correlation

Numeric feature vs. binary Response. Mathematically identical to Pearson r applied to a 0/1 variable.

range −1 to +1

Cramér's V

Education / Marital_Status vs. Response, via chi-square on a contingency table.

range 0 to 1

Threshold |score| ≥ 0.10 to be considered relevant.

08 Classification

Model-Based Importance — Random Forest

Method

RandomForestClassifier(n_estimators=300, random_state=42) fit on all 25 candidates (dummy-encoded categoricals, median-filled Income). Dummy importances summed back to parent columns.

Purpose

Bivariate correlation misses interactions and non-linearities. A tree ensemble can capture what a single-feature score can't.
